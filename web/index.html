Everything You Need to Ace Your Essays

Instant AI Scoring

A DeBERTa-v3 regression pipeline can predict a 1–6 score after you train it on an appropriate dataset.

QWK Evaluation

The training code reports RMSE, quadratic weighted kappa, and accuracy within half a point on its validation split.

Smart Feedback

Simple paragraph, sentence-length, and vocabulary-diversity rules produce general writing suggestions.

Multi-Sample Dropout

During training, predictions from five dropout rates are averaged through a shared regression layer.

Local prototype

The Flask app runs locally. The pretrained model files are downloaded from Hugging Face when first needed.

Real-time Metrics

Epoch metrics are printed during training and saved in a local JSON log.
